1. DEFINITION & PRODUCT USE
   JRT Tech ANALIST Pro is a high-performance, professional native desktop application 
   designed for Honda Matic & Sport motorcycle Engine Control Unit (ECU) diagnostic logging, 
   DTC fault clearance, and K-Line EEPROM/Flash remapping.

2. SAFETY GUARD & ECU PROTECTION
   - Prior to initializing any Flash Read or Write session, ensure the vehicle's 
     battery voltage (Vbat) is equal to or exceeds 11.5 Volts.
   - Do NOT disconnect the USB FTDI interface cable or switch off the main ignition key 
     during an active 5-phase Flash Programming sequence.
   - The application automatically creates a synchronous byte-for-byte binary 
     backup of your ECU contents prior to applying any flash modifications.

3. HARDWARE & K-LINE INTERFACE REQUIREMENTS
   - Standard 5V FTDI FT232RL / FT232H / Prolific USB-to-Serial K-Line Adapter.
   - Baud Rate: 10,400 bps (8-N-1).

4. DISCLAIMER & RESPONSIBILITY
   Remapping fuel, ignition timing, or engine RPM limits alters vehicle operating 
   parameters. JRT Tech Studio provides automated safety verification guards to 
   protect your physical ECU hardware, but tuning values applied to the engine 
   remain the sole responsibility of the user/technician.
